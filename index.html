SymeonKyriakopoulos

Symeon Kyriakopoulos is a London-based Pilates instructor recognised for his precision-led approach to movement, combining clinical expertise with a refined understanding of performance, posture, and intelligent body conditioning.

With over a decade of teaching experience, he has worked across some of London's most respected luxury fitness and wellness spaces, including Equinox, LabSpa, CoreKensington and Rambert Dance Company, building a reputation for highly personalised teaching and exceptional attention to movement quality.

Trusted by executives, creatives, performers, and professionals from the arts and film industries, Symeon is known for delivering technically sophisticated sessions that are both results-driven and deeply tailored to the individual.

Originally trained at the prestigious Rambert School of Ballet and Contemporary Dance with a DADA scholarship, he combines refined movement expertise with extensive academic studies in Psychology (BSc), Ballet and Contemporary Dance (BA), Dance Science (Post Dip), biomechanics, and rehabilitation-focused Pilates.

He is fully certified in STOTT PILATES across the full Pilates apparatus - including Cadillac, Chair, and Barrels - rather than solely Matwork and Reformer, which is often the public perception of Pilates training today. His approach integrates biomechanics, breathwork, posture analysis, strength training, and somatic awareness to support long-term physical resilience, movement efficiency, and sustainable wellbeing.

His sessions are known for their precision, depth of attention, and ability to bridge performance, rehabilitation, and intelligent strength training.
