SymeonKyriakopoulos

Symeon trained at The Rambert School of Ballet and Contemporary Dance and holds a Postgraduate Diploma in Dance Science, a BSc (Hons) in Psychology, and a STOTT Pilates certification.

He is also a qualified Holistic Massage practitioner and a brown belt in modern Wu-Shu. He currently teaches as part of the faculty at Rambert+, the online platform of Rambert.

His sessions draw on biomechanics, somatic awareness, and years of professional movement training — guiding students toward correct alignment with patience and precision.
